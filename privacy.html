Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how MeharFlow Batch Studio ("the extension", "we") handles your information. The extension is made by Mehar Bilal.

What the extension accesses

  • The prompt documents (.docx / .csv) and folders that you choose to open in it.
  • The Flow web page, so it can fill in prompts and read your generation results.
  • The images and videos you generate in Flow, so it can save them to your computer.

Where your data is stored

All settings, shot lists, reference thumbnails, progress and folder permissions are stored locally in your own browser (Chrome storage and IndexedDB). We do not run a server for the extension, and nothing is stored with us.

What is shared

Nothing. The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data, prompts, files, browsing history or account information with us or any third party. It does not use analytics or tracking.

The only network requests it makes are to Flow's own Google domains, using your existing Flow session, to download media you generated.

Purchases

When you buy a licence, payment is handled by our payment provider (merchant of record), which processes your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only what is needed to deliver and support your licence, such as your email address and licence key.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension removes all data it stored in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
