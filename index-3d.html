How it works

Four steps, all inside Chrome's side panel next to Flow.

Install the extension

Add MeharFlow Batch Studio to Chrome and open your Flow project.

Add your references

Upload your characters, locations and props once per episode.

Link your shot list

Pick your episode document, or paste a single prompt.

Press Start

MeharFlow sends each shot with its references and shows the progress on the Flow page.

Questions

Does MeharFlow collect my data?

No. Your prompts, documents, settings and files stay in your own browser. MeharFlow has no server and sends nothing to us. The only outside check is your licence key, with our licence provider. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this made by Google?

No. MeharFlow Batch Studio is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google.

Does it press Generate for me?

For images, it can - or you can switch that off and press it yourself. For video shots you always press Generate yourself.

Which browser does it need?

Google Chrome on a desktop or laptop computer.
